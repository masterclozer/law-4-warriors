Fill Verification - Law 4 Warriors POC (outbound leg)

Master record: demo-master-record.json - veteran James R. Calloway, VA file no. 999-88-7777, decision 2026-10-06 (Waco, Texas), combined 80%.

Method: every filled PDF was re-opened with a fresh pypdf reader and each mapped field's value read back and string-compared to the value the record supplied. Fields are left editable (not flattened) for lawyer review.

Platform caveat (applies to all five forms): the official VA PDFs are hybrid AcroForm/XFA documents. This filler writes the AcroForm layer and sets NeedAppearances, and verification below is an AcroForm read-back. pypdf does not rewrite the embedded XFA dataset; a viewer that renders the XFA layer instead of AcroForm appearances may not show the values. The Acrobat/Adobe route in Stage 2 is the fix if that behavior is observed in the firm's viewer.

The deferred-issue honesty note

The sample record's migraine rating has decision_type deferred - the VA has not yet made a final decision on it, so strictly it is not yet appealable. Per the POC's instructions it is listed on the three decision-review forms (0995/0996/10182) so the mapping is fully exercised; on a real filing the lawyer strikes that row. The fill engine lists exactly what the record says - the lawyer decides what gets filed.
