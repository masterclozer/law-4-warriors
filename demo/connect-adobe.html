Law 4 Warriors

Connect Adobe

This system reads VA rating decision letters with Adobe Acrobat Services (PDF Extract). It ships with no Adobe account attached — the firm connects its own Adobe account here, once. The credentials are encrypted on this server and are used only to sign in to Adobe on the firm's behalf. They are never sent anywhere else.

Connection status

What each state means — the live system shows one of these at a time. (On this preview, “Not configured” is the current sample state; the rest are shown so you can recognize them later.)

Not configured
No Adobe credentials saved yet. The system cannot call Adobe until Step 1 and Step 2 below are done.
CURRENT — SAMPLE
Configured — untested
Credentials are saved, but no successful test yet. Click Test connection to verify them against Adobe.
Connected
Adobe accepted the credentials and issued an access token. Decision letters can be processed. Tokens last 24 hours and refresh automatically.
Authentication failed
Adobe rejected the credentials — usually a typo, a revoked secret, or a credential created in a project without the Acrobat Services API. Re-check and test again.

Step 1Get your credentials from Adobe

You create these once, in the firm's own Adobe account. It takes about five minutes and costs nothing.

  1. Create an Adobe ID with a firm email address (or sign in if the firm already has one).
  2. Go to developer.adobe.com/console and click Create new project — name it “Law 4 Warriors”.
  3. In the project, click Add API and choose Adobe Acrobat Services.
  4. Choose OAuth Server-to-Server as the credential type and save. Adobe shows a Client ID and a Client Secret.
  5. Copy both values into Step 2 below. The full walkthrough, with screenshots described click by click, is in the Adobe Handoff Guide that ships with this system.

Step 2Paste them here

Saved encrypted (Fernet) in adobe-vault.json on this server only. The secret is never displayed again after saving.

COMING IN STAGE 2

Adobe Acrobat Sign

Stage 2 adds e-signature for the forms that need the veteran's signature — VA Form 21-22a (appointment of representative), the fee agreement, and records releases. Acrobat Sign connects the same way: an integration key from the firm's own Acrobat Sign account, pasted here. Nothing to do with this panel yet.

Billing, usage, and the audit trail stay in the firm's name because the account is the firm's. To disconnect Adobe at any time, use “Clear credentials” in the admin tools — or revoke the credential in the Adobe Developer Console, which cuts access immediately. See the Adobe Handoff Guide for rotation and revocation steps.
